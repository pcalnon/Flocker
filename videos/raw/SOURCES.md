# SOURCES — top-down soccer clips (raw)

Provenance manifest for the clips in this directory. Retrieved 2026-11-21 (UTC).

- **10 clips kept**, 684,548,231 bytes in total. Every clip was checked for license, resolution and content, and three frames were inspected (see *Verification*).
- **Sources**: Pexels (8 clips), Pixabay (1), Wikimedia Commons (1).
- **Mix**: 4 full 11-a-side views, 1 near-full view, 1 full-pitch training session and 4 small-sided views (one at night).
- **Files are originals.** Nothing was re-encoded, trimmed or renamed internally, so each SHA-256 below is the hash of the file exactly as served.

### Verification

**License.** I checked each license on the clip's own page on 2026-11-21, through a real browser session.
- **Pexels.** The page's embedded data carries `license: "Pexels"`. The page shows **"License: Free"**, which links to https://www.pexels.com/license/.
- **Pixabay.** The page reads **"Free for use under the Pixabay Content License"**. Its JSON-LD `license` field is https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/.
- **Wikimedia Commons.** The file page states the following, with no deletion or license-review template present: "This file is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 4.0 International license." The downloaded file's SHA-1, `0b2eb0e6537ac8af748ae88b7eeaaeac39cd43d5`, matches the SHA-1 Commons publishes for the file.

**License terms as quoted from the license pages on 2026-11-21:**
- **Pexels:**

  - "Attribution is not required."

  - Not allowed:
    - "Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that is offensive."
    - "Don't redistribute or sell the photos and videos on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms."
    - Plus three rules on resale, endorsement and trademarks.

**Media checks.**
- Every file was checked with `/usr/bin/ffprobe` for duration, resolution, fps and codec.
- Three frames (0.3 s, the middle and 0.6 s before the end) were extracted with `/usr/bin/ffmpeg` and inspected by eye.
- Camera motion was judged from five frames per clip, at 2%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 97% of the duration.

### Notes and caveats

- **Pexels #3 (39134041).** The page lists the source as 2720x1530, but the largest rendition offered is 2560x1440, which is what was downloaded.
- **Pexels #5 (2938865).** This is DCI 4K (4096x2160). I took it as within "up to 4K".
- **Redistribution.** The Pixabay license forbids distributing content "on a Standalone basis", and the Pexels license forbids redistributing on other stock platforms. Before publishing these raw clips as part of a public dataset, check those terms. Sharing source links instead avoids the question.
- **CC BY-SA 4.0 (#10).** Credit is required, and any adapted version, such as a re-encoded or annotated copy, must stay under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible license.
- **People in frame.** Real, mostly unidentifiable players appear in every clip, and no clip states model releases. The Pexels and Pixabay terms forbid portraying identifiable people negatively or deceptively.

**1. SoccerTrack**

Fujii, "SoccerTrack: A Dataset and Tracking Algorithm for Soccer With Fish-Eye and Drone Videos", CVPR Workshops (CVSports) 2022, pp. 3569–3579. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022W/CVSports/html/Scott_SoccerTrack_A_Dataset_and_Tracking_Algorithm_for_Soccer_With_Fish-Eye_CVPRW_2022_paper.html

- **License as Kaggle states it:** "License: GNU Lesser General Public License 3.0". The code repository (https://github.com/AtomScott/SoccerTrack) shows "GPL-3.0 license". Its README states no separate terms for the data.

- **Size:**
  - `top_view/`: 180 files, 5,216,781,441 bytes (`top_view.zip` is 5,190,728,295 bytes):

- **Paths (visible listing):** `top_view/videos/D_20220220_1_0000_0030.mp4` through `top_view/videos/D_20220220_1_1770_1800.mp4`. These are 60 consecutive 30 s segments covering 0–1800 s, about 35 MB each. Matching `top_view/annotations/*.csv` and `top_view/viz_results/*.mp4` files share the same names.
